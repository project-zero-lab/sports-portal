Quick information, basic rules and pre-game checklist for the four selected sports.

General pre-game checklist

  • Check that the playing surface is clean, dry, even and free from obstacles.
  • Inspect all equipment for damage and make sure it is suitable for use.
  • Confirm team size, match format, boundaries and scoring rules before starting.
  • Warm up properly, wear suitable footwear and follow the teacher / referee's instructions.
  • Keep drinking water and a basic first-aid kit accessible; report unsafe conditions.
